# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** built using Blinkit's grocery sales dataset to analyze sales performance, customer ratings, outlet efficiency, and product trends. The dashboard provides actionable business insights through interactive visualizations and KPIs to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Blinkit is one of India's leading quick-commerce platforms. This project analyzes sales data across different outlet locations, outlet sizes, product categories, and customer ratings. The goal is to identify key business trends and help stakeholders make informed decisions regarding inventory, sales strategy, and outlet performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large amounts of sales data, making it difficult to identify high-performing outlets, popular product categories, and customer buying patterns.

This dashboard solves these challenges by providing:

- Sales performance tracking
- Outlet-wise performance analysis
- Product category comparison
- Customer rating analysis
- Interactive filtering for faster business insights

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- Tier 3 outlets generated the highest overall sales.
- Medium-sized outlets contributed significantly to total revenue.
- Fruits, Snacks, and Household items were among the top-selling categories.
- Older outlets consistently showed strong sales performance.
- Customer ratings remained stable across most outlet types.
